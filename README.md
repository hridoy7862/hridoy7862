# 👋 About Me

I am **Md. Reajul Karim Hridoy**, an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from **Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)** with a strong interest in digital hardware and semiconductor design.

My primary interests are:

- RTL Design
- Design Verification
- SoC & CPU Architecture
- FPGA Prototyping
- ASIC Design
- Hardware Security
- Secure SoC Design
- Logic Locking
- Hardware Trojan Detection

I enjoy working close to the hardware — from writing RTL and developing verification environments to FPGA implementation and physical design.
