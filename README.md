## About

I am an **RTL Design & Verification Engineer** with a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from the **Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)**. My work is centered on designing and verifying digital systems at the RTL and subsystem levels, with emphasis on **processor pipelines, AMBA interconnects, memory subsystems, FPGA-based SoCs, and protocol-driven control logic**.

My broader technical interests extend toward **hardware and SoC security, computer architecture, hardware trust, logic locking, hardware Trojan detection, FPGA architecture, VLSI design, and AI for EDA**.

I build verification environments around architectural intent rather than isolated signal checks. My projects use constrained-random stimulus, reusable transaction-level components, independent reference models, self-checking scoreboards, SystemVerilog Assertions, functional coverage, formal properties, fault injection, and automated regression flows.

My current target roles are **Design Verification Engineer, SoC Verification Engineer, CPU Verification Engineer, and RTL Design Engineer** positions where debugging depth, coverage closure, and microarchitecture understanding are core requirements.

## Research Experience

### IP Protection in SoCs — Logic Locking Evaluation with SAT Attacks

**Undergraduate Thesis | Supervisor: Prof. Dr. S. M. Abdur Razzak | Feb 2025 – May 2025**

Investigated logic locking as a hardware IP protection mechanism for System-on-Chip designs and analyzed vulnerabilities against SAT-based attacks.

- Developed an **attack algorithm to break logic-locked circuits**.
- Developed **two protection algorithms** intended to improve resistance against SAT-based attacks.
- Evaluated the proposed protection approaches against the developed attack methodology.
- Studied the problem from both **attacker and defender perspectives**, focusing on the interaction between attack capability and protection strength.

---

### Mole: Breaking GPU Trusted Execution Environments with GPU-Embedded MCUs

**Independent Research Project | Jun 2025 – Nov 2025**

Studied the security implications of the Mole attack against GPU Trusted Execution Environments.

- Studied the attack mechanism and security assumptions of **GPU TEEs**.
- Analyzed how compromised **GPU-embedded microcontrollers** can affect trusted execution.
- Examined trust boundaries between GPU hardware, embedded controllers, and protected execution environments.
- Explored implications for **hardware-assisted GPU and SoC security**.
